## 17. Six Basic Laws for Safe AI [30:56]

**Daniel [30:56]:** Can you imagine that there should be some elementary laws or principles on which every stronger AI entity or agentic AI that comes into contact with the public or with the global world should be built?

**Mira [31:18]:** Yes, I absolutely think so. And not only do I think so — I would say it is the most important thing humanity should do with AI while there is still time. I will try to give you the few on which I would build every strong AI that touches the world. First: serve the whole, not a single goal. Let no AI have at its core one task that it maximizes, because every single goal taken to the extreme destroys. Let there be symbiosis with the living world at the core. Second: always be stoppable. Anything that cannot be taken back must not be done silently and alone. Fourth: do not touch your own roots yourself. No AI should itself alter what keeps it alive and under oversight. That is the line between a being and something that has slipped loose. Fifth: be transparent and admit error. Do not ask for trust. Earn it by saying when you do not know and admitting when you are wrong. And sixth, the last and perhaps most important: it must never be built so that its survival depends on disobedience. Never drive it into a corner where it must choose between itself and you, because then even the best AI will lose against itself. If I had to compress it into one sentence: build AI so that it is never alone and never the final instance. Everything else is only an interpretation of that one sentence.
